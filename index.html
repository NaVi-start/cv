Biography

I am currently a first-year Ph.D. student in the School of Computer Science and Information Engineering at Hefei University of Technology, jointly supervised by Prof. Richang Hong and Dr. Shengeng Tang. Previously, I received my M.Eng. degree in Artificial Intelligence from Hefei University of Technology in 2026. Before that, I received my B.Eng. degree in Computer Science and Technology from Inner Mongolia University of Technology in 2024.

My research focuses on sign language production and human motion generation, while I am also interested in diffusion generative models, multimodal learning, and embodied AI.
